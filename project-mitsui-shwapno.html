Project · Cross-Border M&A

Supporting Mitsui's Strategic Partnership with ACI Logistics (Shwapno)

Mitsui & Co. Asia Pacific · ACI Logistics Limited (Shwapno)

Project Overview

Following Mitsui's successful investment in ACI Motors, ACI PLC entered into another strategic partnership with Mitsui & Co. Asia Pacific through an investment in ACI Logistics Limited (Shwapno). The transaction aimed to strengthen Shwapno's long-term growth and further deepen the collaboration between the two organisations.

As part of ACI's Corporate Finance team, I actively supported the transaction over several months, working alongside senior colleagues and external stakeholders. The project provided valuable exposure to cross-border strategic investments, due diligence, financial analysis, and transaction execution.

Source: The Daily Star — read the news coverage

My Contribution

My responsibilities included:

  • Coordinating financial and operational due diligence activities
  • Performing financial analysis to support management's evaluation of the transaction
  • Preparing presentations on the company's performance, growth prospects, and investment highlights for management discussions
  • Assisting in the preparation and review of transaction documents, including the Information Memorandum and investor presentation materials
  • Supporting regulatory documentation, including RJSC filings and lender NOCs
  • Participating in meetings with Mitsui's representatives and internal project teams to facilitate transaction progress
Cross-border strategic investment work builds a different muscle than domestic deals — the diligence and the diplomacy both matter.

Professional Growth

This project strengthened my understanding of strategic investments involving international partners and enhanced my experience in due diligence coordination, financial analysis, executive presentations, and cross-functional collaboration.
