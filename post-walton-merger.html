High Court approves the Walton merger — a template for faster restructuring

August 2026 · 3 min read

The news: The High Court has approved the merger of non-listed Walton Digi-Tech Industries Ltd into listed Walton Hi-Tech Industries Ltd, with Walton Digi-Tech as the transferor company and Walton Hi-Tech as the transferee. The merger is expected to strengthen Walton Hi-Tech's portfolio through vertical integration, adding laptops, desktop PCs, printers, mobile phones, PCBs, IT accessories, and electric bikes to its lineup, with the company citing cost and operational efficiencies, technological synergies, and a stronger competitive position as the rationale. The board endorsed an MoU on the merger last September, BSEC issued its no-objection certificate in March, and shareholders approved a share exchange ratio of one Walton Hi-Tech share for every 38.45 Walton Digi-Tech shares at an EGM in June — with 7,80,234 Walton Hi-Tech shares to be issued against Walton Digi-Tech's three crore shares.

Source: The Business Standard — read the full report

Why this matters beyond Walton

A significant milestone for corporate restructuring in Bangladesh. The Walton merger demonstrates how restructuring can create operational efficiency, technological synergies and greater business value.

A smoother, faster approval process could turn mergers from a last resort into a standard strategic tool.

The bigger signal

More importantly, a smoother and faster approval process could encourage other companies to explore mergers and demergers as strategic solutions, reducing regulatory complexity and unlocking greater corporate efficiency.
