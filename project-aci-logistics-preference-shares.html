Corporate Restructuring & Preference Share Financing for ACI Logistics

BDT 640Cr + BDT 700Cr Convertible Preference Shares · ACI Logistics (Shwapno)

Project Overview

As part of ACI Logistics' strategic restructuring, the company undertook an internal capital restructuring to improve its financial position ahead of future growth initiatives.

A significant portion of the company's financing consisted of intercompany loans, resulting in substantial interest expenses. To optimise the capital structure, ACI initiated the issuance of convertible preference shares, allowing a portion of the debt to be replaced with equity-like financing. The structure was designed so that dividend payments would only become payable once the business achieved sustainable profitability.

The first phase involved the issuance of BDT 640 crore of convertible preference shares, followed by approval for an additional BDT 700 crore.

Sources: TBS News — Phase 1 (BDT 640Cr)  ·  TBS News — Phase 2 (BDT 700Cr)

My Contribution

I was actively involved in supporting this corporate restructuring initiative through:

  • Conducting financial viability analysis to evaluate the proposed restructuring
  • Assisting in the preparation of term sheets and supporting documentation for the convertible preference share issuance
  • Preparing the Information Memorandum and related financial information for management approval
  • Supporting RJSC processes relating to increases in authorised and paid-up capital
  • Coordinating documentation and other activities required throughout the restructuring process
Replacing intercompany debt with equity-like financing tied to future profitability — a structure built for the business's actual trajectory, not just its balance sheet today.

Professional Growth

This project provided practical experience in corporate restructuring and alternative financing strategies beyond traditional debt and ordinary equity. It enhanced my understanding of capital structure optimisation and the role of preference shares in supporting long-term business growth.
